Services

FJB works in Manhattan apartments, townhouses, homes and commercial spaces. Most of our clients come to us through referrals, and many have been with us for decades.

General Contracting

Full renovations, from demolition to final paint. We coordinate every trade, including electrical, plumbing and millwork.

  • Demolition and site preparation
  • Electrical and plumbing, through licensed trade partners
  • Custom millwork for kitchens and bathrooms
  • Tile and marble installation
  • Marble and stone fabrication
  • Structural steel, wall and glass
  • Hardwood flooring
  • Painting and skim coating

Building Maintenance

Apartment repairs for property managers: water damage, plaster, floors, patching and painting to match, in one unit or several.

  • Water damage repair
  • Plaster, wall and ceiling repair
  • Patching, priming and painting to match
  • Floors: repair, replace, sand, stain and poly
  • Painting and skim coating
  • Cabinet and fixture reinstallation
  • Several units in one building, quoted together
  • Urgent repairs

Construction Management

Budgets, scheduling, quality control, expediting and DOB filing for your project.

  • Budget management
  • Detailed project oversight
  • Scheduling and quality control
  • Product options and selection
  • Expediting and DOB filing
  • Construction design and architectural support
  • Walk-throughs and digitized plan review
